    Outreach & Service

    Science outreach and communication

    ICTP Physics Without Frontiers

    Since 2019 I have been the regional coordinator for the Kurdistan Region of Iraq of Physics Without Frontiers (PWF), the outreach program of the Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP). PWF works to motivate, train and educate university students in physics and mathematics, especially in regions and groups that are poorly represented in science. As coordinator I have organized:

    • 2022: PWF Workshop on the Large Hadron Collider, University of Garmian
    • 2021: 2nd ICTP–PWF Summer School on Particle Physics and Cosmology, Salahaddin University, Erbil, where I lectured on Achievements in Cosmology: An Introduction to the Standard Model of Cosmology
    • 2019: 1st ICTP–PWF School on Particle Physics and Cosmology, University of Sulaimani, where I lectured on Open Issues in Cosmology

    Physickurd

    Since 2014 I have been a writer for Physickurd, an online physics outreach blog that I run with Kurdish colleagues. We write about current scientific achievements in Kurdish to raise public awareness of science.

    Chawg Podcast

    I am a regular guest on the Chawg Podcast, a non-profit Kurdish-language science podcast broadcasting from Copenhagen, Denmark, talking about astronomy and cosmology. Episodes include:

    • Existence and Nothingness (special episode), Jul 2020
    • How is the age of the Universe, stars and planets estimated?, Aug 2019
    • The Big Bang Theory, Jul 2017
    • Scientific awareness and dissemination to raise the quality of thinking, May 2017
